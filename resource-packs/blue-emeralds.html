Blue Emeralds

A small Minecraft resource pack featuring blue-themed emeralds, custom items and a collection of fun textures. It isn't a full visual overhaul — just a bunch of custom items and tweaks.

Version 1.2 Minecraft 1.21+ Pack format 34
Download on Modrinth ↗ Planet Minecraft ↗

What's included?

Blue Emeralds is a small pack with custom models and textures, themed items and a few vanilla item changes.

  • Blue-themed emeralds: custom_emerald1 and custom_emerald2
  • Custom gold ingot: custom_gold_ingot1
  • Carved pumpkin variants: custom_carved_pumpkin1
  • Fun custom items: French Fries, Master Sword, BunnyBux and Money
  • Vanilla changes: Bread, Emerald, Gold Ingot, Iron Sword and Carved Pumpkin
  • Small extras: adjusted pumpkin blur and tutorial textures

Ideal for players who want some fun custom items, or creators and modders looking for assorted item ideas.
